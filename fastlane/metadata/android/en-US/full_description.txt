Open source bitcoin portfolio tracker.

Features:
- Total privacy: Stacks are entered manually and they never leave your phone. OBT is not a wallet, it's a price tracker.
- Fast and lightweight: OBT doesn't connect to a node so it loads fast and has minimal battery impact.
- Handy converter: bitcoin to sats and fiat.
- Multiple languages and currencies.
- Light and Dark modes.
- PIN and biometric lock for your stacks.
- Historical price charts.
- Mempool fee tracker and network hashrate chart.
