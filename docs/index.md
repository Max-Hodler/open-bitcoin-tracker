# Open Bitcoin Tracker

A free, open-source Android app for tracking the Bitcoin price and the value of your stacks — no accounts, no servers, no tracking.

- Live BTC price and multi-currency portfolio value
- Organize your bitcoin into multiple labeled stacks
- Hide the value of your stacks behind a PIN or biometrics
- Mempool feed and network hashrate chart
- Available in Deutsch, English, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Türkçe, Tiếng Việt, Русский, and 日本語, with light and dark themes (including an AMOLED-friendly pure-black option)
